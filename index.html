How do I choose the right platform size?

Compare the 80 cm and 120 cm deck diameters with your aircraft’s landing gear footprint and the clearance required for your operation. Check the selected model’s specifications and your aircraft manufacturer’s guidance before use.

What is the difference between ST and LED?

ST is the standard platform. LED versions add integrated lighting. Both formats are available with 80 cm and 120 cm deck diameters. Check the store listing for the exact equipment supplied with each model.

Where can I order AEROPAD-X?

Order through our dedicated product website, aeropadx.com ↗, where you will find current prices, availability and delivery options.

Can we discuss distribution or a development project?

Yes. Use the contact form on our product website to introduce your business and describe your requirements. For a technical enquiry, include the intended use, aircraft type and any relevant dimensions.
